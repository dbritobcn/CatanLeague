## Why

The league works but its minimal presentation does not express its Catán identity or adapt navigation to touch devices. The user approved Product Design option 2 and its tablet/mobile adaptations.

## What Changes

- Introduce a forest-green sidebar, warm ivory surfaces, Inter typography, supplied emblem/favicon, and illustrated landscape.
- Adapt navigation into a compact rail on tablet and a bottom bar on mobile.
- Redesign standings with clear scores, provisional final positions, real progress and contextual actions.
- Apply the visual system to registration, match scheduling, result editing and completed-league states.
- Improve field labels, feedback announcements, focus visibility and touch targets.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `responsive-league-ui`: Accessible branded league presentation across desktop, tablet and mobile, preserving all existing workflows.

### Modified Capabilities
None. Scoring, scheduling, persistence and domain rules remain unchanged.

## Impact

Next.js layouts/pages, presentation components, global CSS and local assets. Add self-hosted Inter and Phosphor icons. No data migration, new routes, authentication or external API integrations.
